# Performance budgets

- **First Contentful Paint (FCP)**: `<= 900ms` on 4G.
- **Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS)**: `0.00` (explicit `width`/`height` on all media, `font-display: swap`).
- **JavaScript bundle budget**: `<= 120 KB` gzipped for interactive apps; `<= 15 KB` for content/marketing sites.
- **Image budget**: `<= 250 KB` per hero image in WebP/AVIF; zero unoptimized multi-megabyte PNGs.
