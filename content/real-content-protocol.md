# Real content protocol (zero fabrication rule)

1. **Never invent social proof**: Do not fabricate client quotes, star ratings, user counts, partner logos, press mentions, or team bios.
2. **Explicit client markers**: When required data is missing from the brief, insert `[CLIENT INPUT NEEDED: <exact item>]` in the draft and log it in `templates/reports/client-todo.md`.
3. **Structural adaptation**: If a client has zero testimonials and three products, cut the testimonial section and expand the product specification comparison.
