# Headline patterns that read as human

Replace template slogans with four grounded headline structures:

1. **Task + Timeframe / Tolerance**: `Clear imported machinery through Alexandria Port in 48 hours.`
2. **Concrete Mechanism**: `Double-entry bookkeeping connected directly to Egyptian e-Invoice v1.0.`
3. **Audience + Problem Solved**: `Cold-chain temperature logging for pharmaceutical distributors.`
4. **Audited Fact**: `1,840 digitized survey maps of the Nile Delta (1882–1954).`
