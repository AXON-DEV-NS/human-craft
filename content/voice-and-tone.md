# Voice and tone calibration

Define the voice in the project's Direction Statement along three concrete axes:

1. **Register**: Peer practitioner (plain, direct, specific) vs Institutional record (formal, cited, neutral).
2. **Person**: Second-person active (`Calculate your CIF duty`) for tools; first-person plural (`We mill brass fittings to DIN 10204`) for service workshops.
3. **Banned project vocabulary**: List 10 industry clichés specific to the client's sector and block them in `allowlist.json` / project patterns.
