# Copywriting rules

1. **Cut every unbacked adjective**: Replace `seamless`, `robust`, `cutting-edge`, `revolutionary`, and `holistic` with the mechanism, protocol, or measured number.
2. **Ban rhetorical AI crutches**: Never write 'It's not just X, it's Y', 'In today's fast-paced world', 'Whether you're A, B, or C', or 'Say goodbye to X and hello to Y'.
3. **Vary sentence length**: Follow a 22-word technical sentence with a 6-word declarative sentence.
4. **Sentence-case headings**: Capitalize only the first word and proper nouns in all `H1–H4` headings.
