# Bilingual content (Arabic + English)

1. **Parallel authoring, not literal translation**: Write the Arabic copy natively for the target Arab market and the English copy natively for international readers.
2. **Preserve proper nouns and technical codes**: Wrap Latin SKUs, port codes (`EGALY`), and phone numbers inside `<bdi dir="ltr">` when embedded in Arabic sentences.
3. **Matched information density**: Ensure both languages convey the exact same prices, SLAs, and legal terms without leaving untranslated English fallback strings in the Arabic view.
