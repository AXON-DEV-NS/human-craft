# Alt text and figure captions

1. **Never start with `Image of` or `Photo of`**: Screen readers already announce `image`.
2. **Informational images**: Describe the specific action or data (`Technician measuring 18mm steel plate thickness with an ultrasonic gauge`).
3. **Charts and diagrams**: Summarize the trend and endpoints in `alt` (`Bar chart showing port dwell time dropping from 6.4 days in Q1 to 2.1 days in Q4 2025`).
4. **Decorative dividers or background shapes**: Set `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"`.
