# SEO copywriting

- **`<title>` format**: `Specific Page Topic — Organization Name` (45–60 characters). Never leave `Vite + React` or `Home`.
- **`<meta name="description">`**: 120–155 characters stating who the page serves, what concrete action or data it provides, and the geographic or technical scope. Every page must have a unique description.
- **Anchor text**: Internal links must name the destination topic (`Read the 2026 demurrage rate table`) rather than `Read more`.
