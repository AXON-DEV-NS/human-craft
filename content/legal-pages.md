# Legal, privacy, and trust pages

1. **No fake compliance badges**: Never display SOC 2, HIPAA, GDPR, or ISO 27001 logos unless the organization holds a current audit certificate with a verifiable report date.
2. **Concrete privacy disclosures**: Name the actual sub-processors (e.g. Stripe, AWS Frankfurt `eu-central-1`, Postmark) and cookie names rather than pasting a generic `[Company Name]` template.
3. **Missing legal terms**: Mark unverified clauses with `[CLIENT INPUT NEEDED: registered legal entity address and governing court]`.
