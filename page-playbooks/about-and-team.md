# Playbook: About and team page

## 1. Non-template section orders

- **Order A (Proof-first)**: 1. Founding date, city, and specific problem tackled -> 2. Shop floor / office photos -> 3. Named principals with prior engineering/domain roles -> 4. Audited milestones.
- **Order B (Specification-first)**: Open immediately with the interactive filter/table above the fold, followed by methodology and direct action.
- **Order C (Editorial narrative)**: Lead with a documented customer problem and cost breakdown, then show the step-by-step resolution.

## 2. What to cut

- Cut 'We are a passionate team of visionaries', `pravatar.cc` headshots, and generic core-values icon grids.

## 3. Required real content

- Real founder names, authentic photos, year founded, and physical address. (If any item is missing, mark `[CLIENT INPUT NEEDED]` in `client-todo.md`).
