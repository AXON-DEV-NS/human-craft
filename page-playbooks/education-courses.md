# Playbook: Education and course syllabus

## 1. Non-template section orders

- **Order A (Proof-first)**: 1. Syllabus week-by-week curriculum table -> 2. Prerequisites and time commitment -> 3. Instructor credentials and sample lesson -> 4. Tuition and refund policy.
- **Order B (Specification-first)**: Open immediately with the interactive filter/table above the fold, followed by methodology and direct action.
- **Order C (Editorial narrative)**: Lead with a documented customer problem and cost breakdown, then show the step-by-step resolution.

## 2. What to cut

- Cut 'Unlock your true potential' hype and fake countdown timers.

## 3. Required real content

- Module topics, instructor bio, total hours, and exact price. (If any item is missing, mark `[CLIENT INPUT NEEDED]` in `client-todo.md`).
