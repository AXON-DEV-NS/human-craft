# Playbook: Blog and technical article

## 1. Non-template section orders

- **Order A (Proof-first)**: 1. Specific declarative headline + author + date + reading time -> 2. Key finding or benchmark summary -> 3. Evidence, code/charts, and methodology -> 4. Primary source links.
- **Order B (Specification-first)**: Open immediately with the interactive filter/table above the fold, followed by methodology and direct action.
- **Order C (Editorial narrative)**: Lead with a documented customer problem and cost breakdown, then show the step-by-step resolution.

## 2. What to cut

- Cut 'In today's digital landscape' intros, 'In conclusion' wrap-ups, and AI-generated hero illustrations.

## 3. Required real content

- Named human author, publication date, real benchmark numbers, and primary citations. (If any item is missing, mark `[CLIENT INPUT NEEDED]` in `client-todo.md`).
