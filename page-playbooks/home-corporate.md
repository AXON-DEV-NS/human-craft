# Playbook: Corporate home page

## 1. Non-template section orders

- **Order A (Proof-first)**: 1. Clear statement of industrial capability and geography -> 2. Product/service catalog with specs -> 3. Facility & certification proof -> 4. Direct dispatch/sales contact.
- **Order B (Specification-first)**: Open immediately with the interactive filter/table above the fold, followed by methodology and direct action.
- **Order C (Editorial narrative)**: Lead with a documented customer problem and cost breakdown, then show the step-by-step resolution.

## 2. What to cut

- Cut 'In today's fast-paced world' manifesto, stock office photos, and '10K+ happy clients' counters.

## 3. Required real content

- Registered company name, physical plant/office locations, ISO/regulatory licences, and real leadership contacts. (If any item is missing, mark `[CLIENT INPUT NEEDED]` in `client-todo.md`).
