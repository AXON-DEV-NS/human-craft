# Playbook: E-commerce storefront & checkout

## 1. Non-template section orders

- **Order A (Proof-first)**: 1. Filterable category catalog with visible specs -> 2. Transparent cart summary with shipping/tax before account creation -> 3. Single-screen guest checkout.
- **Order B (Specification-first)**: Open immediately with the interactive filter/table above the fold, followed by methodology and direct action.
- **Order C (Editorial narrative)**: Lead with a documented customer problem and cost breakdown, then show the step-by-step resolution.

## 2. What to cut

- Cut newsletter popups on first load, spin-to-win wheels, and hidden shipping fees.

## 3. Required real content

- Unit prices, delivery timeframes by region, supported payment methods, and return address. (If any item is missing, mark `[CLIENT INPUT NEEDED]` in `client-todo.md`).
