# Playbook: Portfolio and case study

## 1. Non-template section orders

- **Order A (Proof-first)**: 1. Client name, sector, date, and measured outcome -> 2. Constraints & legacy bottleneck -> 3. Architecture/design decisions made -> 4. Before/after metrics.
- **Order B (Specification-first)**: Open immediately with the interactive filter/table above the fold, followed by methodology and direct action.
- **Order C (Editorial narrative)**: Lead with a documented customer problem and cost breakdown, then show the step-by-step resolution.

## 2. What to cut

- Cut tilted 3D mockups floating on neon blobs and vague 'We elevated their brand' claims.

## 3. Required real content

- Client permission, project dates, team roles, and real deliverables. (If any item is missing, mark `[CLIENT INPUT NEEDED]` in `client-todo.md`).
