# Playbook: Product detail page

## 1. Non-template section orders

- **Order A (Proof-first)**: 1. High-resolution multi-angle media + key spec summary -> 2. Dimension/compatibility table -> 3. Shipping & warranty terms -> 4. Verified buyer reviews.
- **Order B (Specification-first)**: Open immediately with the interactive filter/table above the fold, followed by methodology and direct action.
- **Order C (Editorial narrative)**: Lead with a documented customer problem and cost breakdown, then show the step-by-step resolution.

## 2. What to cut

- Cut fake '27 people are viewing this right now' urgency timers and generic marketing adjectives.

## 3. Required real content

- SKU code, dimensions, materials, weight, stock status, and return window. (If any item is missing, mark `[CLIENT INPUT NEEDED]` in `client-todo.md`).
