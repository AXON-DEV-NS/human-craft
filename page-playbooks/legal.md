# Playbook: Privacy policy and terms of service

## 1. Non-template section orders

- **Order A (Proof-first)**: 1. Plain-language summary table -> 2. Exact data collected and named sub-processors -> 3. Retention periods and deletion contact -> 4. Governing law and entity address.
- **Order B (Specification-first)**: Open immediately with the interactive filter/table above the fold, followed by methodology and direct action.
- **Order C (Editorial narrative)**: Lead with a documented customer problem and cost breakdown, then show the step-by-step resolution.

## 2. What to cut

- Cut `[Insert Company Name]` brackets and contradictory boilerplate clauses.

## 3. Required real content

- Legal entity name, registration number, data controller email, and jurisdiction. (If any item is missing, mark `[CLIENT INPUT NEEDED]` in `client-todo.md`).
