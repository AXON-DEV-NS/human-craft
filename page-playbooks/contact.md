# Playbook: Contact and quote request page

## 1. Non-template section orders

- **Order A (Proof-first)**: 1. Direct email, phone, and physical address with response SLA -> 2. Short 4-field quote/support form -> 3. Regional office hours and map coordinates.
- **Order B (Specification-first)**: Open immediately with the interactive filter/table above the fold, followed by methodology and direct action.
- **Order C (Editorial narrative)**: Lead with a documented customer problem and cost breakdown, then show the step-by-step resolution.

## 2. What to cut

- Cut dead `action="#"` forms, `alert('Thank you!')` handlers, and `123 Main St` addresses.

## 3. Required real content

- Monitored email address, working phone number, street address, and expected reply window. (If any item is missing, mark `[CLIENT INPUT NEEDED]` in `client-todo.md`).
