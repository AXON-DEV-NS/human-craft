# Playbook: Restaurant and local business

## 1. Non-template section orders

- **Order A (Proof-first)**: 1. Today's hours, neighborhood address, and reservation/order action -> 2. Full priced menu in HTML text (not just a blurry PDF) -> 3. Kitchen/venue photos.
- **Order B (Specification-first)**: Open immediately with the interactive filter/table above the fold, followed by methodology and direct action.
- **Order C (Editorial narrative)**: Lead with a documented customer problem and cost breakdown, then show the step-by-step resolution.

## 2. What to cut

- Cut 'A symphony of flavors' clichés and Unsplash burger photos.

## 3. Required real content

- Exact street address, opening hours, dish prices, and allergen notes. (If any item is missing, mark `[CLIENT INPUT NEEDED]` in `client-todo.md`).
