# Playbook: Nonprofit and civic initiative

## 1. Non-template section orders

- **Order A (Proof-first)**: 1. Concrete mission, region served, and audited impact ratio -> 2. Field program breakdown -> 3. Financial transparency & annual reports -> 4. Donation allocation table.
- **Order B (Specification-first)**: Open immediately with the interactive filter/table above the fold, followed by methodology and direct action.
- **Order C (Editorial narrative)**: Lead with a documented customer problem and cost breakdown, then show the step-by-step resolution.

## 2. What to cut

- Cut 'A beacon of hope' rhetoric and unverified impact counters.

## 3. Required real content

- Registration number, audited financial breakdown, and field locations. (If any item is missing, mark `[CLIENT INPUT NEEDED]` in `client-todo.md`).
