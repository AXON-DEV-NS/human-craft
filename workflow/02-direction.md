# Phase 2 — Direction Statement and archetype selection

**Required output**: `templates/direction/direction-statement.md`

## Steps

1. Select one of the 12 archetypes in `references/directions/README.md` suited to the client's industry and audience.
2. Complete a one-page `Direction Statement` (`templates/direction/direction-statement.md`) locking exact font files, modular scale ratio, 60/30/10 hex tokens, grid column split, imagery rule, motion curves, and copy register.
3. If building programmatic video or canvas animation, complete `templates/direction/storyboard.md` first.
