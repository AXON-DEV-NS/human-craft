# Copywriting overview (one-page hub)

Write with nouns, verbs, and verified numbers. Cut every adjective that cannot be backed by a measurement or mechanism.

## Core rules

1. **Specifics over hype**: Replace 'Seamless integration' with 'Connects to SAP Business One via read-only ODBC in 4 minutes'.
2. **Varied sentence cadence**: Mix 6-word declarative punches with 22-word technical explanations. Avoid rule-of-three adjective stacks and 'Not just X, it's Y' contrasts.
3. **Native Arabic writing**: Choose the target register in the Direction Statement (Simplified MSA, Egyptian, Gulf, Levantine, or Maghrebi) and write natively—never translate English marketing idioms word for word.
