# Limits and ethics

1. **Zero fabricated social proof**: Never invent testimonials, client logos, star ratings, user numbers, or certifications. Use `[CLIENT INPUT NEEDED: ...]` and track missing items in `client-todo.md`.
2. **Preserve legal provenance**: Never strip C2PA or content-credential provenance metadata where law, platform policy, or contract requires disclosure. `scripts/strip-metadata.sh` keeps provenance intact by default.
3. **Honest scope**: Static scanners catch deterministic tells; human review of the Direction Statement and rendered viewports remains essential.
