# 12 — Hand-Crafted Editorial Illustration

## 1. Typography

- **Latin pairing**: Fraunces + Plus Jakarta Sans
- **Arabic pairing**: El Messiri + Readex Pro
- **Scale & measure**: See `references/design/typography.md` (`tabular-nums` required on all figures).

## 2. Color logic

- **Palette tokens**: Cream `#fbf8f1`, Ink `#1c1917`, Marigold `#d97706`, Teal `#0f766e`, Ink Border `#1c1917`
- **Distribution**: 60% primary paper surface, 30% primary ink and structural borders, 10% single accent.

## 3. Grid and spatial rhythm

- **Structure**: Storybook modular panels with crisp `1.5px solid #1c1917` ink borders
- **Vertical pacing**: Alternate compact data sections (`3rem`) with open narrative pauses (`6rem`).

## 4. Imagery rule

- Custom ink-and-wash or flat vector spot illustrations with consistent character proportions

## 5. Motion rule

- 200ms subtle rotation/offset on interactive stickers; respects reduced-motion

## 6. Copy voice

- **Tone**: Warm, witty, concrete, never corporate

## 7. Fit and anti-fit

- **Best-fit industries**: Independent restaurants, bookstores, elementary education, local festivals
- **When NOT to use**: Enterprise security compliance
