# 04 — Tactile Workshop & Craft

## 1. Typography

- **Latin pairing**: Fraunces + Work Sans
- **Arabic pairing**: El Messiri + Almarai
- **Scale & measure**: See `references/design/typography.md` (`tabular-nums` required on all figures).

## 2. Color logic

- **Palette tokens**: Warm Linen `#f7f3eb`, Espresso Ink `#211c18`, Terracotta `#b4532a`, Border `#dfd7c8`
- **Distribution**: 60% primary paper surface, 30% primary ink and structural borders, 10% single accent.

## 3. Grid and spatial rhythm

- **Structure**: Asymmetric 8-column workshop layout with generous margin notes
- **Vertical pacing**: Alternate compact data sections (`3rem`) with open narrative pauses (`6rem`).

## 4. Imagery rule

- Close-up macro photography of raw materials, wood grain, textiles, or roasted beans

## 5. Motion rule

- Gentle `220ms cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` drawer reveals

## 6. Copy voice

- **Tone**: Sensory, provenance-focused, naming makers and origins

## 7. Fit and anti-fit

- **Best-fit industries**: Specialty coffee roasters, furniture makers, ceramics, culinary workshops
- **When NOT to use**: High-frequency trading platforms
