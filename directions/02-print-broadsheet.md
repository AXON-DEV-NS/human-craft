# 02 — Print Broadsheet & Gazette

## 1. Typography

- **Latin pairing**: Newsreader + Source Sans 3
- **Arabic pairing**: Noto Naskh Arabic + IBM Plex Sans Arabic
- **Scale & measure**: See `references/design/typography.md` (`tabular-nums` required on all figures).

## 2. Color logic

- **Palette tokens**: Newsprint `#f4efe6`, Carbon `#181715`, Deep Burgundy `#7c1d24`, Rule `#cfc8bc`
- **Distribution**: 60% primary paper surface, 30% primary ink and structural borders, 10% single accent.

## 3. Grid and spatial rhythm

- **Structure**: Multi-column editorial layout with `1px` column rules and double-rule section headers
- **Vertical pacing**: Alternate compact data sections (`3rem`) with open narrative pauses (`6rem`).

## 4. Imagery rule

- Halftone or duotone archival photography with detailed dated captions

## 5. Motion rule

- Static print stillness; instant page state changes

## 6. Copy voice

- **Tone**: Journalistic, cited, narrative-driven

## 7. Fit and anti-fit

- **Best-fit industries**: Think tanks, legal practices, investigative journalism, literary publishers
- **When NOT to use**: Real-time telemetry dashboards
