# 09 — Calm Utility & Personal Tool

## 1. Typography

- **Latin pairing**: Plus Jakarta Sans + Atkinson Hyperlegible
- **Arabic pairing**: Readex Pro + Vazirmatn
- **Scale & measure**: See `references/design/typography.md` (`tabular-nums` required on all figures).

## 2. Color logic

- **Palette tokens**: Warm Oat `#f5f2eb`, Soft Charcoal `#1e1d1b`, Sage `#2f5d48`, Border `#ded9ce`
- **Distribution**: 60% primary paper surface, 30% primary ink and structural borders, 10% single accent.

## 3. Grid and spatial rhythm

- **Structure**: Focused single-column workspace (`max-w-3xl`) with tactile `6px`-radius controls
- **Vertical pacing**: Alternate compact data sections (`3rem`) with open narrative pauses (`6rem`).

## 4. Imagery rule

- Clean inline SVG workflow previews that respond to user input

## 5. Motion rule

- Interruptible spring transitions (`180ms`) on toggles and drawers

## 6. Copy voice

- **Tone**: Encouraging, plainspoken, zero jargon

## 7. Fit and anti-fit

- **Best-fit industries**: Personal finance, scheduling tools, patient portals, study apps
- **When NOT to use**: Heavy industrial machinery
