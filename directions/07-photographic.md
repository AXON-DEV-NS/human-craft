# 07 — Documentary Photographic

## 1. Typography

- **Latin pairing**: Instrument Serif + Source Sans 3
- **Arabic pairing**: Amiri + Almarai
- **Scale & measure**: See `references/design/typography.md` (`tabular-nums` required on all figures).

## 2. Color logic

- **Palette tokens**: Darkroom Matte `#161514`, Warm Silver `#f0ede8`, Olive `#4d6b53`, Border `#2e2c29`
- **Distribution**: 60% primary paper surface, 30% primary ink and structural borders, 10% single accent.

## 3. Grid and spatial rhythm

- **Structure**: Full-bleed documentary photo essays paired with `60ch` field-note columns
- **Vertical pacing**: Alternate compact data sections (`3rem`) with open narrative pauses (`6rem`).

## 4. Imagery rule

- Original location photography with EXIF focal length and location notes in captions

## 5. Motion rule

- Respectful stillness; no ken-burns pan or parallax hijacking

## 6. Copy voice

- **Tone**: Observational, grounded in place and date

## 7. Fit and anti-fit

- **Best-fit industries**: Field NGOs, agriculture, marine shipping, regional tourism, documentary film
- **When NOT to use**: Abstract API documentation
