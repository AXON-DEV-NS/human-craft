# 08 — High-Density Operational Ledger

## 1. Typography

- **Latin pairing**: IBM Plex Sans + IBM Plex Mono
- **Arabic pairing**: IBM Plex Sans Arabic + IBM Plex Mono
- **Scale & measure**: See `references/design/typography.md` (`tabular-nums` required on all figures).

## 2. Color logic

- **Palette tokens**: Ledger White `#f8f9fa`, Slate Ink `#0f172a`, Forest Positive `#15803d`, Crimson Alert `#b91c1c`
- **Distribution**: 60% primary paper surface, 30% primary ink and structural borders, 10% single accent.

## 3. Grid and spatial rhythm

- **Structure**: Full-viewport split panes, compact `32px` table rows, keyboard shortcut badges (`J`/`K`)
- **Vertical pacing**: Alternate compact data sections (`3rem`) with open narrative pauses (`6rem`).

## 4. Imagery rule

- Sparklines, status dots with text labels, and diff tables; zero decorative illustrations

## 5. Motion rule

- Zero entrance animation; instant `<50ms` keyboard selection movement

## 6. Copy voice

- **Tone**: Terse, tabular, unit-explicit (`EGP`, `kg`, `ms`)

## 7. Fit and anti-fit

- **Best-fit industries**: Accounting software, customs brokers, server observability, medical lab portals
- **When NOT to use**: Brand storytelling sites
