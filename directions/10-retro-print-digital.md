# 10 — Technical Archive & Microfiche

## 1. Typography

- **Latin pairing**: Space Mono + Work Sans
- **Arabic pairing**: IBM Plex Sans Arabic + Space Mono
- **Scale & measure**: See `references/design/typography.md` (`tabular-nums` required on all figures).

## 2. Color logic

- **Palette tokens**: Manila Folder `#f3eedc`, Stamp Black `#191816`, Postal Blue `#1e3a8a`, Rule `#b8b09c`
- **Distribution**: 60% primary paper surface, 30% primary ink and structural borders, 10% single accent.

## 3. Grid and spatial rhythm

- **Structure**: Stamped index cards, tabular catalog numbers (`REF-884-B`), perforated borders
- **Vertical pacing**: Alternate compact data sections (`3rem`) with open narrative pauses (`6rem`).

## 4. Imagery rule

- Scanned blueprints, patent drawings, and dithered monochrome plates

## 5. Motion rule

- Discrete stepped frame transitions (`steps(4)`)

## 6. Copy voice

- **Tone**: Archival, cataloged, precise

## 7. Fit and anti-fit

- **Best-fit industries**: Engineering archives, open-source hardware, audio synthesis tools, independent presses
- **When NOT to use**: Corporate banking
