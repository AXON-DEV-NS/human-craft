# 01 — Editorial Swiss Grid

## 1. Typography

- **Latin pairing**: Instrument Serif + IBM Plex Sans
- **Arabic pairing**: Amiri + IBM Plex Sans Arabic
- **Scale & measure**: See `references/design/typography.md` (`tabular-nums` required on all figures).

## 2. Color logic

- **Palette tokens**: Paper `#f6f4f0`, Ink `#141413`, Vermilion `#c83e2b`, Hairline `#d6d1c7`
- **Distribution**: 60% primary paper surface, 30% primary ink and structural borders, 10% single accent.

## 3. Grid and spatial rhythm

- **Structure**: 12-column exposed `1px` hairline grid with asymmetric `4 + 8` splits
- **Vertical pacing**: Alternate compact data sections (`3rem`) with open narrative pauses (`6rem`).

## 4. Imagery rule

- High-contrast documentary photography or technical line drawings; zero stock office photos

## 5. Motion rule

- 150ms opacity/color transitions only; zero scroll-triggered entrance slides

## 6. Copy voice

- **Tone**: Crisp, factual, numbered clauses

## 7. Fit and anti-fit

- **Best-fit industries**: Architecture firms, research institutes, financial infrastructure, design studios
- **When NOT to use**: Children's games or casual entertainment
