# 05 — Gallery & Exhibition Catalog

## 1. Typography

- **Latin pairing**: Syne + Plus Jakarta Sans
- **Arabic pairing**: Reem Kufi + Vazirmatn
- **Scale & measure**: See `references/design/typography.md` (`tabular-nums` required on all figures).

## 2. Color logic

- **Palette tokens**: Alabaster `#faf9f6`, Charcoal `#161615`, Ochre `#9a6b26`, Hairline `#e5e2dc`
- **Distribution**: 60% primary paper surface, 30% primary ink and structural borders, 10% single accent.

## 3. Grid and spatial rhythm

- **Structure**: Wide whitespace margins (`15%` viewport framing) with offset caption columns
- **Vertical pacing**: Alternate compact data sections (`3rem`) with open narrative pauses (`6rem`).

## 4. Imagery rule

- Un-cropped full-frame artwork or architectural plates with catalog numbers

## 5. Motion rule

- Slow `320ms` cross-fades when switching plates

## 6. Copy voice

- **Tone**: Curatorial, restrained, date-and-medium annotated

## 7. Fit and anti-fit

- **Best-fit industries**: Museums, cultural foundations, interior architecture, photography portfolios
- **When NOT to use**: High-density B2B ERP forms
