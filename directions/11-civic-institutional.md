# 11 — Civic & Public Service Standard

## 1. Typography

- **Latin pairing**: Source Sans 3 + Source Serif 4
- **Arabic pairing**: Noto Sans Arabic + Noto Naskh Arabic
- **Scale & measure**: See `references/design/typography.md` (`tabular-nums` required on all figures).

## 2. Color logic

- **Palette tokens**: Public White `#ffffff`, High-Contrast Ink `#0b0c0c`, Civic Blue `#1d70b8`, Focus Yellow `#ffdd00`
- **Distribution**: 60% primary paper surface, 30% primary ink and structural borders, 10% single accent.

## 3. Grid and spatial rhythm

- **Structure**: Clear step-by-step task lists, prominent search, `65ch` service guides, zero marketing fluff
- **Vertical pacing**: Alternate compact data sections (`3rem`) with open narrative pauses (`6rem`).

## 4. Imagery rule

- Clear instructional diagrams only where needed to explain a physical document

## 5. Motion rule

- Zero decorative motion; strict WCAG AAA focus visibility

## 6. Copy voice

- **Tone**: Plain language, action-oriented, eligibility-first

## 7. Fit and anti-fit

- **Best-fit industries**: Municipal services, hospitals, universities, utilities, legal aid
- **When NOT to use**: Fashion lookbooks
