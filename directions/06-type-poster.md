# 06 — Typographic Poster & Monograph

## 1. Typography

- **Latin pairing**: Familjen Grotesk + Instrument Sans
- **Arabic pairing**: Readex Pro (700) + Noto Sans Arabic
- **Scale & measure**: See `references/design/typography.md` (`tabular-nums` required on all figures).

## 2. Color logic

- **Palette tokens**: Stark Bone `#f2efe9`, Pitch Ink `#111110`, Cobalt `#1d4ed8`, Rule `#111110` (`2px`)
- **Distribution**: 60% primary paper surface, 30% primary ink and structural borders, 10% single accent.

## 3. Grid and spatial rhythm

- **Structure**: Oversized typographic index numbers (`clamp(3rem, 7vw, 6.5rem)`) anchoring asymmetric blocks
- **Vertical pacing**: Alternate compact data sections (`3rem`) with open narrative pauses (`6rem`).

## 4. Imagery rule

- Typographic diagrams and high-contrast monochrome portraits

## 5. Motion rule

- Snappy `140ms` horizontal rule expansions on focus/hover

## 6. Copy voice

- **Tone**: Bold, thesis-driven, zero filler adjectives

## 7. Fit and anti-fit

- **Best-fit industries**: Conferences, music venues, independent software studios, campaign microsites
- **When NOT to use**: Multi-thousand-SKU e-commerce catalogs
