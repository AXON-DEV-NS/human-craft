# Moodboard and reference sampling protocol

1. **Sample outside web templates**: Draw at least two references from physical artifacts in the client's field (e.g., printed customs waybills, train timetables, machine nameplates, or architectural blueprints).
2. **Extract measurable tokens**: Record the exact paper tint hex, rule thickness (`1px` vs `2px`), and numeral alignment rather than vague mood words.
