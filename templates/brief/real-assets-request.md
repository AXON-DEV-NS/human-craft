# Real assets request

Send the following original files before launch so no stock filler is shipped:

1. **Vector wordmark and emblem**: `.svg` or `.eps` in primary ink and monochrome reverse.
2. **Location / product photography**: Uncropped `.jpg` or `.png` (minimum `2400px` wide) of actual facilities, equipment, or products.
3. **Named customer quotes**: Full name, job title, company name, and written approval for each quote.
4. **Legal entity details**: Registered company name, commercial register number, tax card number, and street address.
