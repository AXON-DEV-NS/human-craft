# Color systems and 60/30/10 palettes

Never use raw Tailwind `indigo-500` (`#6366f1`), `violet-500` (`#8b5cf6`), or `pink-500` (`#ec4899`). Build every palette from three warm or cool neutrals plus one domain accent.

## Verified production palettes

| Palette | 60% Surface (`--surface-paper`) | 30% Ink (`--color-ink`) | Muted Ink (`--color-muted`) | 10% Accent (`--color-accent`) | Hairline (`--border-subtle`) | Contrast (Ink/Paper) |
